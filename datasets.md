# Recommended Datasets for Portfolio Projects

To make this portfolio truly "complete," you can use the following high-quality, real-world datasets from open-source platforms like Kaggle and GroupLens.

## 1. Large-Scale Recommendation System
*   **MovieLens 100k / 1M / 20M**: The gold standard for recommender systems research.
    *   [Download from GroupLens](https://grouplens.org/datasets/movielens/)
*   **Amazon Product Data**: Contains millions of reviews and category metadata.
    *   [Link to Dataset](https://nijianmo.github.io/amazon/index.html)

## 2. End-to-End Fraud Detection
*   **IEEE-CIS Fraud Detection**: A massive real-world dataset from a payment service.
    *   [Kaggle Link](https://www.kaggle.com/c/ieee-fraud-detection)
*   **Credit Card Fraud Detection**: Highly imbalanced dataset using PCA-transformed features.
    *   [Kaggle Link](https://www.kaggle.com/mlg-ulb/creditcardfraud)

## 3. A/B Testing & Experimentation Platform
*   **A/B Test Marketing Group**: Dataset to test the effectiveness of a marketing campaign.
    *   [Kaggle Link](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/faviovaz/marketing-ab-testing)
*   **E-commerce Conversion Data**: Standard user-level conversion logs for statistical testing.
    *   [Kaggle Link](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/zhangluyuan/ab-testing)

## 4. NLP Sentiment & Review Analytics
*   **Yelp Dataset**: Millions of reviews for local businesses, perfect for BERT training.
    *   [Official Yelp Dataset](https://www.yelp.com/dataset)
*   **Amazon Fine Food Reviews**: Over 500,000 reviews from 1999 to 2012.
    *   [Kaggle Link](https://www.kaggle.com/snap/amazon-fine-food-reviews)

## 5. Time Series Sales Forecasting
*   **Walmart Recruiting - Store Sales Forecasting**: Predict weekly sales across departments and stores.
    *   [Kaggle Link](https://www.kaggle.com/c/walmart-recruiting-store-sales-forecasting)
*   **Store Item Demand Forecasting**: 5 years of daily sales for 10 stores.
    *   [Kaggle Link](https://www.kaggle.com/c/demand-forecasting-kernels-only)

## 6. Data Pipeline & MLOps
*   **California Housing Prices**: Great for small-scale pipeline automation demo.
    *   [Scikit-Learn Resource](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.datasets.fetch_california_housing.html)
*   **NYC Taxi Fare Prediction**: High-throughput data for engineering robust pipelines.
    *   [Kaggle Link](https://www.kaggle.com/c/new-york-city-taxi-fare-prediction)

## 7. RAG Retrieval & Evaluation
*   **SQuAD (Stanford Question Answering Dataset)**: Ideal for testing retrieval-augmented responses.
    *   [Official Explorer](https://rajpurkar.github.io/SQuAD-explorer/)
*   **ArXiv Metadata**: Over 1.7 million scholarly articles in PDF format.
    *   [Kaggle Link](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/Cornell-University/arxiv)

## 8. Customer Churn Prediction
*   **Telco Customer Churn**: Subscription features with Yes/No churn label (IBM / Kaggle standard).
    *   [Kaggle Link](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/blastchar/telco-customer-churn)
*   **IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition**: HR-style “attrition” as a churn analogue.
    *   [Kaggle Link](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/pavansubhasht/ibm-hr-analytics-attrition-dataset)
